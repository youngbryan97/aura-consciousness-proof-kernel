# How this demo relates to the current ISC research

Status: Guide · Checked 25 October 2026

The main Aura project asks whether its memory, attention, self-state, planning
and other measured parts form one continuing process. ISC is the 24-check
experiment used to investigate that organisation. This repository contains an
older demonstration of a few components, rather than that experiment.

## A later isolation repair limits the recorded results

At `dcdcd637c`, the main project found that experimental arms shared a record
of consequences. One comparison could therefore affect a later comparison.
The completed scores below remain recorded outputs, but the paired findings
need rechecking with that history properly isolated. The
[source-hashed isolation update](https://github.com/youngbryan97/aura/blob/main/docs/evidence/ISC_ISOLATION_UPDATE_2026-10-25.json)
records the repair and distinguishes its diagnostic from a fresh campaign.

At `3e9211d3f`, a second shared record was repaired: recent degradation reports
could affect the next arm's threat computation. The repaired-source pre-flight
kept the unchanged state identical after sixty cuts and a clock shift, while
its planted positive control moved the state. This is not a fresh campaign.
The main status page also records that the derived-margin calculation exposed
an estimator failure on the earliest fold of a developing run; naming the
margin rule did not solve that measurement problem.
