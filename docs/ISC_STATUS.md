# How this demo relates to the current ISC research

Status: Guide · Checked 25 October 2026

The main Aura project asks whether its memory, attention, self-state, planning
and other measured parts form one continuing process. ISC is the 24-check
experiment used to investigate that organisation. This repository contains an
older demonstration of a few components, rather than that experiment.

## A later isolation repair limits the recorded results

At `dcdcd637c`, the main project found that experimental arms shared a record
of consequences. One comparison could therefore affect a later comparison.
The completed scores below remain recorded outputs, but the paired findings
need rechecking with that history properly isolated. The
[source-hashed isolation update](https://github.com/youngbryan97/aura/blob/main/docs/evidence/ISC_ISOLATION_UPDATE_2026-10-25.json)
records the repair and distinguishes its diagnostic from a fresh campaign.

## Latest checked result from the main repository

- The completed seed-23 run at `8f548ed79` passed 22/24 under ISC-v6 and 21/24
  under v5. The four joint-effect comparisons passed within the v6 campaign.
- Separate content and withheld self-report experiments passed. The self-report
  result used the real 27B model and an experimental layer-43 basis. The stored
  report accepted 29 fully readable anchors out of 80 collected.
- Two battery checks failed after an unchanged comparison drifted enough to
  trigger the split-system test. The carrier report and completed J* join
  returned `NOT_FOUND` and `NO_CARRIER` respectively, with four bridge grounds
  holding and the carrier ground failing.
- Source at `cf0ace7e3` distinguishes an open boundary from a question the
  measurement cannot settle. Its preregistered rule derives the closure margin
  from the least informative internal domain. The revised method and repaired
  isolation need fresh confirmation; they do not rewrite the old verdict or
  make 22/24 a full pass. See the
  [rule update](https://github.com/youngbryan97/aura/blob/main/docs/evidence/ISC_CLOSURE_RULE_UPDATE_2026-10-25.json).

22/24 is a count of operational checks. It is not a percentage of consciousness.
